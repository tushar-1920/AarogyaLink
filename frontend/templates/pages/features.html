✨ Full Feature List

Everything AarogyaLink
Can Do

A complete platform for rural health records, telemedicine, ASHA worker management, and government-ready analytics. All in one QR code.

📋 Patient Registration & Records
🎤

Voice Registration in Hindi

Speak patient details in Hindi or English. The form fills automatically using the Web Speech API. Works on Chrome and Edge browsers.

📸

Live Photo Capture

Camera opens in the browser. Circular crop applied. Photo stored on the server and displayed on the patient's health card and profile.

🔲

QR Code Generation

Unique QR code generated in under 1 second. Links to the patient's profile URL. Scannable on any phone without any app installation.

📄

PDF Health Card

A printable PDF with patient photo, QR code, blood group, and critical conditions. Print at any local shop for ₹3. Laminate for lifetime use.

📁

Document Upload

Upload previous lab reports, X-rays, prescriptions, scans, or discharge summaries. View, download, or delete from the patient profile.

🔍

Advanced Patient Search

Search by name, patient ID, phone number, village, or district. Filter by blood group, condition, or last visit date.

🩺 Doctor Features
📷

QR Scanner

Built-in QR code scanner using the phone camera. Scan a patient card and see their complete medical history in under 3 seconds.

🎤

Voice-Dictated Visits

Doctor speaks diagnosis, prescription, and notes in Hindi or English. Text auto-fills the form. Each voice click appends — doesn't overwrite.

⚠️

Allergy Alert Banner

Red animated banner appears immediately when viewing a patient with known allergies. Impossible to miss before prescribing.

📊

Doctor Analytics

Monthly patient count, top diagnoses, follow-up compliance rate, busiest day of week, and 7-day visit trend chart.

📋

Prescription Templates

Save frequently-used prescriptions as one-click templates. Saves 3-4 minutes per patient on documentation.

📤

Patient Referrals

Refer a patient to another doctor or hospital. System generates a referral letter PDF with complete patient history attached.

🎥 Telemedicine & Online Consultations New
👤

Online Patient Accounts

Patients create separate online accounts with live photo, Aadhar verification, and medical history. Auto-approved instantly — no admin wait.

📅

Doctor Slot Management

Doctors generate time slots by selecting date, start time, end time, and duration per patient. System auto-creates all slots.

💳

UPI Payment with QR

Doctor's UPI QR code shown on payment page. Patient pays and uploads screenshot. Doctor verifies and confirms the appointment.

🎥

Jitsi Video Calls

Powered by Jitsi Meet — completely free, no account needed. Unique room per appointment. Works on any browser, any device.

💬

Post-Call Prescriptions

After the call, doctor sends clinical notes and prescription digitally. Patient sees it in their Messages section instantly.

🕐

Real-time Availability

Doctor's profile shows live availability — slots today, this week. Green/Yellow/Red indicator based on actual available slots.

👩‍⚕️ ASHA Worker Features
🎯

Target Tracker

Supervisor sets monthly targets — registrations, home visits, IFA tablets distributed. ASHA sees progress bars in real time.

📓

Daily Activity Log

Replaces the paper diary. Log home visits, medicines distributed, counselling sessions. Supervisor views from admin panel.

🏆

Leaderboard & Badges

District leaderboard of top ASHA workers by registrations. Milestone badges at 10, 50, 100, and 500 patients. Shareable digital certificate.

💬

ASHA to Doctor Messaging

ASHA attaches a patient record and sends a clinical question to the assigned doctor. Doctor replies with advice within hours.

🗺️

Village Coverage Map

Leaflet.js map showing registered patients as dots. Uncovered areas clearly visible. Plan daily route to maximize coverage.

📱

Offline PWA

Install on Android home screen. Works without internet all day. Auto-syncs when connectivity returns. No data lost ever.

⚙️ Admin & Security Features
🛡️

Document Verification

Admin reviews Aadhar, medical degree, and council certificate before approving any doctor or ASHA account.

📜

Full Audit Trail

Every view, edit, login, and approval logged with timestamp and IP address. Tamper-proof. Required for hospital licensing.

📊

District Analytics

Real-time charts: patient registrations over time, top conditions by district, visit trends, blood group distribution, follow-up rates.

🔐

DPDPA Compliance

Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 compliant. Explicit consent at registration. Patient can revoke at any time.

📡

Real-time Notifications

Live toast notifications when a doctor accesses a patient's record. ASHA workers see activity on their patients in real time.

🌐

Hindi / Punjabi UI

One-click language toggle switches the entire interface to Hindi or Punjabi. Preference saved in browser storage. Works immediately.
