Privacy Policy

How AarogyaLink collects, uses, and protects your personal health data. We take privacy seriously — especially for sensitive medical information.

Last updated: May 2026 · Version 1.0
Table of Contents
1. What Data We Collect 2. How We Use Your Data 3. How We Store and Protect Data 4. Who We Share Data With 5. Your Rights Under DPDPA 2023 6. Consent and Withdrawal 7. Children's Data 8. Contact Our Data Officer
📋
1. What Data We Collect

Patient Data (collected by ASHA workers)

  • Full name, age, gender, date of birth
  • Phone number and emergency contact
  • Village, district, state
  • Blood group, known allergies, current medications
  • Medical conditions and diagnoses
  • Photo (live capture at registration)
  • Uploaded medical documents (lab reports, X-rays, prescriptions)
  • Aadhar card number (last 4 digits only for verification)
  • Visit history added by verified doctors

Doctor/ASHA Account Data

  • Full name, email, phone number
  • Medical license number, speciality, hospital name
  • Aadhar card, medical degree, council certificate (for verification)
  • Login timestamps and IP addresses

Online Consultation Data

  • Appointment symptoms and medical notes
  • Payment screenshots (UPI confirmation)
  • Video call room IDs (no recordings are stored)
  • Post-call prescriptions and messages
✅ We do NOT collect GPS location. We do NOT record video calls. We do NOT use cookies for advertising. We do NOT sell your data to anyone.
🔍
2. How We Use Your Data
  • To provide healthcare continuity: Patient data is shown to verified doctors who scan the QR card, enabling informed medical treatment without repeated paperwork.
  • To generate health cards: Patient name, photo, QR code, and basic info is used to create printable PDF health cards.
  • To send reminders: Phone numbers are used to send SMS follow-up reminders and medication alerts.
  • To generate analytics: Anonymised, aggregated data is used to show district-level health trends to government health officers. No individual patient is identifiable.
  • To verify accounts: Doctor and ASHA documents are used solely for verifying that they are legitimate healthcare professionals.
⚠️ We never use patient data for marketing, advertising, or any commercial purpose beyond the healthcare services described above.
🛡️
3. How We Store and Protect Data
  • Encryption in transit: All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS 1.3. No data travels unencrypted.
  • Server security: Hosted on Render.com with SOC 2 compliance. Regular security patches applied.
  • Access controls: Only verified, approved doctors can view patient records. ASHA workers can only see patients they registered. Admin access is logged and audited.
  • Audit trails: Every access to a patient record is logged with user ID, timestamp, and IP address. Tampering is impossible.
  • Uploaded documents: Stored in isolated folders with randomized filenames. Not publicly guessable URLs.
  • Data retention: Patient records are retained permanently as long as the patient consents. Account data for doctors/ASHA is retained for 5 years after account closure per medical record keeping regulations.
🤝
4. Who We Share Data With

We Share With:

  • Verified doctors who scan the patient's QR card — they see the patient's full medical history to provide treatment.
  • Government health departments receive only anonymised, aggregate reports — no individual patient data.
  • Render.com (our hosting provider) processes data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements.

We Never Share With:

  • Pharmaceutical companies or advertisers
  • Insurance companies (without explicit patient consent)
  • Any third party for commercial purposes
  • Law enforcement without a valid court order
✅ We follow data minimisation principles. We only share the minimum data necessary for the specific purpose.
⚖️
5. Your Rights Under DPDPA 2023

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data. We will anonymise or delete within 30 days (some data may be retained for legal compliance).
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: File a complaint with our Data Protection Officer. We respond within 15 business days.
  • Right to Nominate: You can nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@aarogyalink.in

✅
6. Consent and Withdrawal

Consent is taken at the point of patient registration. The ASHA worker reads the consent statement to the patient or their guardian. Consent is recorded with a timestamp.

You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us. Upon withdrawal:

  • Your data will be anonymised — your identity removed but medical history retained for public health purposes.
  • Your QR card will stop working for doctor access.
  • Your name and contact details will be permanently deleted within 30 days.
⚠️ Withdrawing consent means doctors will no longer be able to access your medical history. This may affect your future healthcare quality.
👶
7. Children's Data

For patients under 18 years of age, consent must be obtained from a parent or legal guardian. The ASHA worker must confirm that the parent/guardian is present and has verbally consented before registration.

Children's health data receives the same security protections as adult data. Under DPDPA 2023, processing of children's data requires parental consent and is prohibited for tracking, targeted advertising, or behavioural monitoring purposes — none of which AarogyaLink does.
