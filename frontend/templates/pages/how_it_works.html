📋 Complete Guide

How AarogyaLink
Works — Step by Step

From a 2-minute registration in a remote village to a doctor scanning a QR card at a district hospital — here's every step of how AarogyaLink gives rural India a lifelong health record.

1
Step One
ASHA Worker Registers the Patient
An ASHA worker visits the patient's home or health camp. She opens AarogyaLink on her Android phone — even without internet — and registers the patient by speaking in Hindi.
🎤
Voice input in Hindi: Speak the patient's name, age, village, blood group, and medical conditions. No typing needed.
📵
Offline-first: Works without internet. All data is stored locally and syncs when connection is available.
📸
Live photo capture: Camera opens directly in the browser — no app needed. Photo is captured, cropped as circle, and stored.
⏱️
Total time: Under 2 minutes for a complete registration with photo, conditions, allergies, and emergency contact.
2
Step Two
System Creates the Health Record Instantly
The moment the patient is registered, AarogyaLink automatically generates a unique ID, a QR code PNG, and a printable PDF health card — all in under 3 seconds.
🆔
Unique patient ID: Format RH-XXXXX — random, memorable, and collision-free. Assigned permanently.
🔲
QR code generated: Links directly to the patient's profile URL. Scannable on any smartphone browser.
📄
PDF health card: A4 or credit-card sized PDF with patient photo, QR code, blood group, and key conditions ready to print.
3
Step Three
Patient Receives Their Laminated QR Card
The ASHA worker or health centre prints the PDF health card — costs ₹3 at any local print shop — laminates it, and gives it to the patient. This card is theirs forever.
💰
Cost: ₹3 — A standard black-and-white print. The patient pays nothing. The card is free.
♾️
Never changes: The QR code always points to the same profile. Even as records grow, the card stays the same.
🚨
Emergency info visible: Blood group and allergy alert shown without any scan — printed on the card face.
4
Step Four
Doctor Scans QR — Full History in Seconds
At any hospital, PHC, or clinic in India, the doctor opens their phone camera or the AarogyaLink scan page and points it at the patient's QR card. Full medical history loads instantly.
📱
No app needed: Works in any smartphone browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox. Doctor just needs internet.
⚡
Instant access: Patient name, age, blood group, all conditions, allergies, medications, previous visits — all visible.
🔴
Allergy alert banner: Red warning banner appears prominently if patient has any known allergies — impossible to miss.
5
Step Five
Doctor Adds Visit — Record Grows Permanently
After examining the patient, the doctor adds the visit directly to AarogyaLink using voice dictation or typing. Diagnosis, prescription, notes, and follow-up date are saved permanently.
🎤
Voice dictation: Doctor speaks the diagnosis and prescription in Hindi or English — text fills automatically.
💊
Prescription stored: Every medicine prescribed is saved. Future doctors see full medication history before prescribing.
📅
Follow-up reminder: Set a follow-up date → patient gets SMS reminder automatically. Improves compliance.
🔒
Immutable record: Visit records cannot be deleted. Full audit trail with timestamp and doctor ID.
Three roles

Who uses AarogyaLink

Each user has a different dashboard tailored to their needs.

👩‍⚕️
ASHA Worker
Registers patients via voice, uploads documents, prints QR cards, tracks follow-ups, logs daily activities. Offline-first dashboard with target tracking.
🩺
Doctor
Scans QR cards, reads full history, adds visits with voice dictation, generates online consultation slots, conducts video calls, sends prescriptions digitally.
⚙️
Admin
Verifies doctor and ASHA accounts with document review, manages online patient accounts, views district analytics, monitors platform health.
