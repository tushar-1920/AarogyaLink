👩‍⚕️ ASHA Worker Guide

AarogyaLink ASHA Worker
Complete Guide

Step-by-step instructions for ASHA workers to register patients, upload documents, and manage health records. Available in Hindi and English.

📱
Step 1: Getting Started
First time setup — takes 5 minutes
1
Open your browser Open Chrome or any browser on your phone. Go to the AarogyaLink website. Bookmark it for quick access later.
2
Login with your account Enter your email and password given by your supervisor. If you don't have one, ask your district health officer to register you.
3
You will see your ASHA Dashboard The dashboard shows your patient count, today's registrations, follow-up reminders, and quick action buttons.
💡
Tip: Install AarogyaLink as a PWA. Tap the three-dot menu in Chrome → "Add to Home Screen". The app will appear like a regular app and works offline.
📋
Step 2: Registering a Patient
2 minutes per patient — works without internet
1
Click "Register Patient" from your dashboard or the navigation menu. The registration form opens.
2
Take a photo first Click "Open Camera". Position the patient's face in the circle. Click "Capture". If bad, click "Retake". Then click "Save Photo".
3
Use voice input Click the 🎤 button next to "Patient Name". Speak the name clearly in Hindi or English. The form fills automatically. Do the same for other fields.
4
Fill all fields Name, Age, Gender, Blood Group, Phone, Village, District, State, Emergency Contact, Medical Conditions, Allergies, and Current Medications.
5
Upload old reports (optional) If the patient has old prescription or lab report, take a photo of it and upload. This helps doctors see past medical history.
6
Click "Register Patient" The system creates the record, generates QR code, and creates the PDF health card. This takes 2-3 seconds.
💡
Tip: If the patient doesn't know their blood group, leave it blank. It can be updated later. If no medical conditions, type "None". Never leave fields completely empty.
🖨️
Step 3: Printing the Health Card
Give the patient their permanent QR card
1
Open the patient profile After registration, you are taken to the patient's profile page automatically.
2
Click "Download Health Card PDF" The PDF downloads to your phone. Send it to WhatsApp on a phone connected to a printer.
3
Print at local shop Any local print shop can print for ₹3-5. Ask for lamination (₹5-10 extra) so the card lasts many years without damage.
4
Give card to patient Teach the patient: "Show this card at ANY hospital. The doctor will scan it and see everything. Never throw this away."
📅
Step 4: Managing Follow-ups
Track patients who need follow-up visits
1
Check your dashboard daily The "Pending Follow-ups" section shows patients whose follow-up date has arrived or passed.
2
Visit or call the patient Remind them to visit the PHC. If they are very sick, accompany them.
3
Update the record After the follow-up visit is done, the doctor at the PHC will add the new visit. You don't need to do anything — it updates automatically.
⚠️
Important: Never skip follow-up reminders for pregnant women, diabetic patients, or children under 5. These are high-priority cases.
📓
Step 5: Daily Activity Log
Replace your paper diary with the digital log
1
At end of each day open the "Daily Log" section from your dashboard.
2
Enter today's activities: Number of home visits, patients seen, IFA tablets given, ORS packets given, counselling sessions done.
3
Submit the log. Your supervisor can see this from the admin panel. It auto-generates your monthly report.
✅ Always Do This
✅ Take a clear face photo in good light
✅ Fill allergies section — even if "None"
✅ Note emergency contact number
✅ Upload old reports when available
✅ Log daily activity before sleeping
✅ Tell patient to keep card safe always
❌ Never Do This
❌ Register fake or dummy patients
❌ Share your login password with anyone
❌ Leave the conditions field blank
❌ Register the same patient twice
❌ Delete any patient record
❌ Skip high-risk follow-up reminders
📱 शुरुआत कैसे करें
कदम 1: पहली बार AarogyaLink कैसे खोलें
पहली बार सेटअप — 5 मिनट लगेंगे
1
Chrome ब्राउज़र खोलें अपने Android फोन में Chrome खोलें और AarogyaLink की website खोलें। पेज को Bookmark कर लें।
2
Login करें अपना email और password डालें जो आपके supervisor ने दिया है। अगर account नहीं है तो district health officer से बनवाएं।
3
Dashboard देखें Login के बाद आपका ASHA Dashboard दिखेगा। इसमें आपके patients, follow-up reminders और quick buttons होंगे।
📋 मरीज़ रजिस्ट्रेशन
कदम 2: मरीज़ का रजिस्ट्रेशन कैसे करें
हर मरीज़ के लिए सिर्फ 2 मिनट — बिना internet के भी काम करेगा
1
"Register Patient" पर click करें Dashboard से या menu से "Register Patient" button दबाएं।
2
पहले फोटो लें "Camera खोलें" button दबाएं। मरीज़ का चेहरा circle में रखें। "Capture" दबाएं। अगर फोटो अच्छी नहीं है तो "Retake" करें।
3
🎤 Microphone से भरें "मरीज़ का नाम" के पास 🎤 button दबाएं। हिंदी में बोलें — जैसे "रमेश कुमार" — form खुद भर जाएगा।
4
सभी जानकारी भरें नाम, उम्र, लिंग, Blood Group, फ़ोन नंबर, गाँव, जिला, राज्य, Emergency नंबर, बीमारी, एलर्जी, दवाइयां।
5
पुराने कागज़ upload करें (optional) अगर मरीज़ के पास पुराना prescription या report है तो उसकी फोटो खींचकर upload करें।
6
"Register Patient" दबाएं 2-3 seconds में QR code और health card PDF तैयार हो जाएगा।
🖨️ QR Card प्रिंट करें
कदम 3: QR Health Card कैसे प्रिंट करें
मरीज़ को उसका permanent health card दें
1
Patient profile पर जाएं Registration के बाद profile page अपने आप खुलती है।
2
"Health Card PDF Download" करें PDF आपके phone में download होगी। WhatsApp पर printer वाले phone को भेज दें।
3
Local shop से print करवाएं कोई भी print shop ₹3-5 में print करेगी। ₹5-10 में lamination भी करवाएं ताकि card सालों तक चले।
4
मरीज़ को card दें और समझाएं कहें: "यह card हमेशा अपने पास रखो। किसी भी hospital में doctor को दिखाओ — वो scan करके सब देख लेंगे।"
✅ जरूरी बातें
हमेशा याद रखें
ये गलतियां कभी न करें
✅
फोटो साफ और अच्छी रोशनी में लें। धुंधली फोटो doctors के लिए काम नहीं आती।
✅
एलर्जी का field जरूर भरें — चाहे "कुछ नहीं" लिखना पड़े। यह जानकारी doctor की जान बचा सकती है।
✅
एक ही मरीज़ को दो बार register मत करें। पहले search करें।
❌
अपना login password किसी को मत बताएं — न family को, न दूसरे ASHA worker को।
❌
Pregnant women और बच्चों के follow-up कभी skip मत करें।
