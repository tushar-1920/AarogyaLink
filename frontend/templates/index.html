AI symptom checker

AI reads patient history and current symptoms, flags dangerous drug interactions, and gives triage guidance before the doctor visit begins.

Offline-first PWA

Installs on any Android phone home screen. Works completely without internet. Syncs automatically when connected. Rural coverage = 100%.

QR card in 30 seconds
